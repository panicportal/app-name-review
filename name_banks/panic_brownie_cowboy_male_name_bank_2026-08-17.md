## Research anchors

The bank was built around recognizable Western/cowboy sources, with the **National Cowboy & Western Heritage Museum** as the main authority:

- Great Western Performers: https://nationalcowboymuseum.org/collections/awards/western-performers/inductees/
- Hall of Great Westerners: https://nationalcowboymuseum.org/collections/awards/great-westerners/inductees/
- Library of Congress — Tom Mix: https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/2003666668/

Project duplicate/history sources:
- `name_curation_2026-08-16.json`
- latest Name Studio state/screenshots
- current master naming prompt

The National Cowboy Museum describes its Great Western Performers as performers whose film, television and stage work kept the American West in the public imagination. That makes the Hall a strong first-name source for Brownie cowboy.
